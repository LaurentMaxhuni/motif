Brand research

Your website, read closely.

Choose 6, 12 or 20 pages. Motif finds them through your sitemap and links, then pulls out what your brand already says about itself.

  • Company and products: nested product data, product groups and service candidates.
  • Audience and voice: explicit audience wording, CTA language and tone, quoted from source.
  • Visual identity: palette, fonts and logo declarations from your pages and stylesheets.
  • SEO audit: metadata, headings and structured data issues, plus coverage gaps.
  • Optional interpretation: your chosen text model can read the evidence and suggest audience and voice. It's labelled as inferred, never mixed with observed facts.

Sites that only render with JavaScript may need details entered by hand. You can always skip research and write your Brand DNA yourself.

Creative studio

A brief in, a finished design out.

In HTML/CSS + image mode, your text model writes the copy and designs a fresh composition, and your image model paints the artwork. Motif checks the result and renders it locally as a 1080 × 1080 creative. No canvas editor is needed.

  • Five formats: social posts, ads, emails, SEO articles and video scripts.
  • Batches you can leave: see the current stage, cancel, or retry a failed batch with the same brief and dates. Drafts are saved together once the whole batch is ready.
  • Text stays text: artwork never contains lettering, so copy stays editable and sharp.
  • Offline templates: four original layouts when you'd rather not use a model, clearly labelled as templates.

Revisions & history

Say what you'd change. Keep what you had.

Open a creation and describe the change. Copy and layout are revised together. Tick Generate new artwork to replace the image, or leave it to keep the artwork you like.

  • Undo revision restores the previous preview instantly.
  • Version history lets you preview earlier saved copy and artwork, and Restore as draft without losing the current version.
  • Safe by default: a revision returns to Draft and clears its schedule, so nothing changed goes out unreviewed.
  • Exports: PNG for a ready-to-post creative, standalone HTML with artwork embedded, and the full copy as Markdown.

Planning

A week of content, at your pace.

Every piece moves through Draft, Approved, Scheduled and Ready. The local calendar shows what's going out and when. Plan-a-week fills five days with drafts for you to review.

  • Drafts, never auto-posts: planning creates drafts. It never publishes.
  • Honest numbers: activity shows real counts of what you've made, not invented reach or revenue.
  • Assistant: a brand-aware chat that answers using your saved Brand DNA.

Social connections

Hand off to the tools you already use.

Connect Buffer, Metricool or Zernio per brand. Test & load channels checks your connection without creating a post. When content is approved, choose an account and send it as a draft or an explicit schedule.

  • Reviewed before sending: the creation and the connection are version-checked, and identical deliveries are suppressed.
  • No blind retries: if a result is uncertain, Motif asks you to check the provider before trying again.
  • Delivery history: the creation, account, outcome and remote ID for every send.

Models & providers

Your models, your choice.

Motif doesn't bundle or download a model. Point it at what you already run. Local endpoints must be loopback addresses, and cloud providers are used only when you pick them.

Local text

  • Ollama
  • LM Studio
  • llama.cpp
  • Unsloth

Any OpenAI-compatible /chat/completions server on your machine that supports JSON output.

CLI text

  • Claude Code
  • Codex
  • OpenCode
  • Pi
  • OMP

Use a coding CLI you're already signed in to. Motif checks the install and tests the login before saving. These may use their own cloud services.

Images

  • Cloudflare FLUX.1 Schnell
  • Unsloth Studio
  • Custom local URL

Any OpenAI-compatible /images/generations server, with optional API-key auth. PNG, JPEG and WebP accepted.

Workspace

Everything else a studio needs.

  • Multiple brands, each with its own Brand DNA, voice, audience, palette, product photos and connections.
  • Guided onboarding for each new business: research or manual entry, review, optional model setup, first campaign.
  • Light, dark and system appearance, remembered per browser. Exports keep your brand colours either way.
  • Backups: download a consistent JSON backup while running, export a CSV, and import backups additively.
  • Conflict protection: stale edits are caught before they overwrite newer work.
  • Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile, keyboard-friendly and reduced-motion aware.
