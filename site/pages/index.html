How it works

From brand to campaign, in one place.

Motif doesn't hand you an empty canvas. It starts from what your brand already says, and you review every step before anything is saved or sent.

01

Find your voice

Point Motif at your website. It reads up to 20 public pages and drafts your Brand DNA: products, audience, voice, palette and fonts. Every finding links back to the page and the words it came from.

  • Respects robots.txt and never runs the site's scripts
  • Observed facts kept apart from AI interpretation
  • Nothing is saved until you review and apply it

  • Offline templates when you don't want to use a model

03

Make it yours

Ask for a change the way you would ask a designer. Motif revises the copy and layout together, keeps or replaces the artwork, and saves every version so you can go back.

  • Undo a revision, or restore any saved version as a draft
  • Export PNG, self-contained HTML, or the full copy as Markdown
  • Approve, schedule, and send to your social tools as a draft

Private by design

Local by default. Cloud only when you say so.

Motif runs as a small server on your computer and only listens to it. Nothing reaches the internet until you pick a cloud model, analyze a website, or send a post.

Read how your data is handled
  • Your workspace is a file you own

    Brands, drafts, versions and the calendar live in a SQLite database on your disk. Back it up, move it, or export it as JSON whenever you like.

  • Credentials are encrypted at rest

    Connector and image-provider keys are sealed with AES-256-GCM using a key that stays next to your data and is never included in exports.

  • No telemetry, no remote fonts, no silent fallback

    If a model fails, you see the error. Motif never quietly switches to a cloud service or swaps in template output.

  • Sandboxed rendering

    Generated designs are validated and rendered in an isolated local browser with scripts off and network blocked.

Get started

Two commands to your own studio.

You need Node.js 22.13 or newer, pnpm, and Chrome or Edge for rendering. Then install, start, and open the address it gives you. First launch takes you through brand setup.
